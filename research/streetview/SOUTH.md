# Sector V street survey: south and east (TIU → PwC More → Street 13 → College More)

Surveyed 2026-11-09 from Google Street View and top-down satellite. **All Street View imagery is dated Jan 2026**
(shown in the Google header on every pano). Satellite tiles are older: they still show the SW corner of PwC More
as a vacant plot, but in Street View there is a finished tower there.

Images are in `img/south/` (reference only, gitignored). Names: `svNN_<place>_hHHH.jpg` = Street View at heading HHH
(0 = north). `sat275_*` = top-down satellite at about 275 m. `map_*` = road map. `srch_*` = Google search result.

**Not captured:** the tilted 3D view. The `…,60t/data=!3m1!1e3` URL and Ctrl-drag both fell back to flat
satellite, so Google seems to have no 3D mesh for Sector V (or it wouldn't load in this browser). Building
heights below come from counting floors in Street View, at about 3.5 m a floor for offices and 3 m for older
blocks. Treat them as ±1–2 floors.

Coordinates are (lat, lng). Game units = metres × 0.5 from origin 22.57700, 88.42935 (x east, z south), as in
`tools/build-map.py`.

## Things everywhere in this area (use them as the base kit)

- **Kerbs** are painted in **blue-and-white blocks** (dark navy and white), not black-and-white. They're on medians and footpaths everywhere (`sv21_st13east_h027`, `sv104_st13_h207`).
- **Footpath railings**: blue-painted steel pedestrian barriers along the kerb (blue posts, white/grey rails), about 1 m tall. They run along most compound frontages on Street 13 and Street 18 (`sv105_st13_h027`, `sv108_st13_h207`, `sv41_st18mid_h290`).
- **Compound walls**: a low pastel wall (pink/salmon or cream, about 1 m) with a black or red-brown iron railing on top (about 1.2 m more). Big trees inside. The government institutes use this almost everywhere (CSTARI, Bose Institute, ERTL).
- **Street lights**: decorative **three-lantern "trident" lamp posts** (white globes on curled arms, about 5 m) along footpaths. Taller plain poles with flat LED heads sit on the medians. A **high-mast floodlight** stands at PwC More.
- **Trees**: big old rain trees, banyans, peepal and mango over the footpaths. Lots of areca and royal palms and cypress-like conifers inside compounds. The canopy often covers the whole footpath.
- **Vehicles in frame**: yellow-and-green CNG autos (by far the most common), green totos (e-rickshaws), a blue cycle-rickshaw, colourful private buses (blue and yellow "Maa Chandi"-style livery, faded green-and-white old buses), blue-and-white WBTC buses, white app cabs (Dzire/Etios), white Force Traveller "Uber Shuttle" vans, and many parked scooters and motorbikes.
- **Overhead wires**: there are few on Street 13 and Street 2 (lighting cables only). There are more at College More and on Street 18 (cables across the junction, a telecom mast).
- **Party flags and posters** are tied to median railings and trees on Street 2. The game should use generic coloured pennants, never real party symbols or politicians.

## 1. TIU gate, Street No. 2 (start point)

Pano at 22.57598, 88.42873. Street 2 runs about NNE–SSW (bearing about 18°). TIU is on the **west** side.

**Road.** Divided road, 2 lanes each way (about 6.5–7 m a side), no lane markings. The median is about 1.5 m
wide: a **green-painted pipe railing that looks like bamboo** (three rails, X-braced) with shrubs inside and
blue-and-white kerbs (`sv01_tiugate_h018`, `sv01b_tiugate_fov60_h080`). Tall plain light poles stand in the
median. On the TIU side, cars and a long row of motorbikes are parked nose-in along the kerb
(`sv01b_tiugate_fov60_h250`).

**West side (TIU), from south to north**
- About 22.5753–22.5756: **TIU main entrance**: a pillared portico with a "Techno India Group" sign and a red "T" logo on a pillar. Coconut and areca palms in front. Glass-and-white tower above (`sv00_st2south_h288`, `sv00_st2south_h018`).
- 22.5756–22.5762: **TIU main block**: a grey-clad 2-storey podium with a white-and-glass tower over it, about 10–12 floors (about 40 m), with a **rounded glass corner drum** on the north end (`sv01_tiugate_h288`, `sv01b_tiugate_fov60_h300`). The frontage has a **blue iron railing on brick-red pillars**, a white fence in front of that, and university **standees and banners** ("Keynote Speaker", "Tutorial session") along the railing. A narrow footpath runs between the cars and the fence. Students gather on the road edge, because the parked cars take the footpath.
- North of TIU, 22.5763–22.5768: a **blue corrugated-sheet hoarding** about 2.5 m tall, with scrub behind it. Behind that rises a **new tower of about 20–22 floors (about 75 m)**: peach/salmon cladding with vertical glass strips, and it looks complete (`sv02_st2mid_h288`). It fills the **SW corner of PwC More** (see §3). The satellite tile still shows this as an empty plot.
- Off Street 2 to the west, behind TIU, Google pins "Tushar Tea Stall" (22.5764, 88.4278 roughly; `sat275_a_tiu_pwc`). It is **not on Street 2**; I couldn't see it from the road.

**East side (opposite TIU)**
- From about 22.5754 to 22.5767 (about 150 m) there is a **near-continuous row of hawker stalls**. This is the students' food strip and the most recognisable thing on the street (`sv01_tiugate_h108`, `sv02_st2mid_h108`, `sv00_st2south_h108`).
  - The stalls are single-storey sheds about 3 m deep and 2.5–3 m high. **Corrugated tin and tarpaulin roofs** (rusty brown, faded blue and cream canvas) sit on bamboo or timber posts.
  - A **white tubular railing** (about 1 m) runs in front of the stalls along the kerb, with gaps for entry.
  - The stalls back onto a compound wall (concrete, with slotted vents near the south end) under big rain trees and coconut palms.
  - You can see bananas hanging, tea counters, plastic stools, signboards and a yellow-umbrella ice-cream cart.
  - Roughly 12–15 stall units between TIU and PwC More, and more south of the TIU entrance.
- About 22.5760, almost exactly opposite the TIU gate: an **arched gateway**, a blue-grey arch on brick pillars, that breaks the stall row. It leads into the compound behind, probably the CSTARI campus (`sv01b_tiugate_fov60_h130`).

**Distances.** TIU gate to the PwC More stop line is about 110 m. The TIU frontage is about 100 m long.

## 2. Street 2: TIU → PwC More

## 3. PwC More (Street 13 × Street 2), 22.57691, 88.42923

A **T-junction**: Street 13 runs WNW–ESE (bearing about 117°/297°) and Street 2 comes in from the SSW. There
is no fourth arm going north. The north side is a footpath with a traffic booth.

**Corners and edges**
- **North side, opposite Street 2**: a **traffic police booth**, a blue-and-white striped kiosk about 2.5 m tall with a lit school-ad box on its roof, on a small fenced island by the north kerb. A signal post sits next to it. There's a small fenced patch with a big tree and hoardings (`sv03_pwcmore_h026`).
- **NE corner**: the **PwC office**: a **low white building (2–3 floors)** with the orange "pwc" sign on its parapet. It sits behind a **white boundary wall about 2 m tall with a hedge on top**. People stand along this wall. Behind and east of it is a 6–8 floor glass office with a telecom mast on its roof (`sv03_pwcmore_h026`, `sv03_pwcmore_h116`). (The game POI `pwc` is at 22.57753, 88.42967, about 70 m NNE. The sign and wall in Street View are right on the corner, so the POI could move about 40 m south-west.)
- **NW corner**: a gated compound with a red-brown fence and large trees. **Tin-roofed tea stalls** stand along its fence on the north footpath, west of the booth (`sv03_pwcmore_h296` right side). Per the satellite labels, Ambuja Neotia EcoCentre and AltAir/Capella are further back. I didn't get a clear Street View of them.
- **SE corner** (between Street 2 and the east arm of Street 13): a **small blue building (2 floors) with a black-and-white chequerboard band** on its lower floor, signboards on top, and scaffolding beside it. It looks like a police or traffic-guard post. Behind it is the CSTARI compound (red-brown fence, big trees). Stalls continue round the corner into Street 2 (`sv03_pwcmore_h116`, `sv101_st13_h207`).
- **SW corner**: the new **peach-and-glass tower of about 20–22 floors**. In front, under trees, is an **auto stand**: a queue of yellow-and-green autos and totos along the south kerb of the west arm, with drivers standing about. A **kerbed traffic island** with a blue railing, a signal and a hoarding ("Yatri Sathi" app ad) splits the west-arm traffic (`sv03_pwcmore_h296`).

**Crowd spots.** The mouth of Street 2 (students crossing), the auto stand at the SW corner, and the tea stalls on the north footpath.
**Bus stop.** I saw no shelter at PwC More. Buses stop on Street 13 near the junction.

## 4. Street 13: PwC More → College More (about 520 m, ESE)

**Cross-section.** 2 lanes each way, about 7 m a side. The median varies: a kerbed island near PwC, then a **3–4 m grass

trident lamps and blue barrier railings. In places there's a strip of red bollard bases, where the bollards have

## 5. College More (Street 13 × Street 18), 22.57479, 88.43376

A full **four-way crossroads** with signals. Google's label "College More" is at 22.57475, 88.43376 (`srch_collegemore`).
It's named after the **IEM (Institute of Engineering & Management) "Gurukul Campus College More"** on the SE corner.

- **NW corner**: the HDFC Bank building (white, red portal) (`sv20_collegemore_h027`, `sv20_collegemore_h297`).
- **NE corner**: a **tall glass tower of about 20 floors** with blue-green glazing. A signal post with a countdown timer stands at the corner (`sv20_collegemore_h027`).
- **SE corner**: **IEM building**: a glass curtain-wall block of 6+ floors with a blue band reading "INSTITUTE OF ENGINEERING & MANAGEMENT" and a purple "IEM" sign at street level. Food stalls under trees in front. Students everywhere (`sv20_collegemore_h117`).
- **SW corner**: a black railing with a painted (graffiti/mural) wall, Bose Institute beyond (`sv20_collegemore_h297`).
- **Traffic police kiosk** in the median of the east arm, with a hoarding on top (`sv20_collegemore_h117`). A traffic volunteer in an orange vest works mid-junction.
- **Street 18 north** of the junction: divided with a hedge median. Signet Tower (DN-2, about 12 floors, yellow and glass) is on the west side. A pale-green 4-storey older building with blue trim is on the east side, with a billboard on scaffolding and a telecom mast. A yellow sign reads "**ALL VEHICLES STOP HERE**", a Nabadiganta Traffic Guard bus-stop board fixed to a median pole, with no shelter (`sv22_st18north_h110`, `sv22_st18north_h290`, `sv22_st18north_h200`).
- **Street 18 south** (`sv41_st18mid_*`): a steel median barrier and big hoardings on blue-and-white poles. A government compound with conifers on the west side.
- **Crowd and food**: the SE corner (IEM), the south-side bus shelter just west of the junction, and stalls at the NE corner along Street 18 with banners ("Kolkata Saras Mela" etc.).
- **East of College More** (`sv21_st13east_*`): Street 13 gets **narrower**, about 1 lane plus parking each way, with a palm-and-hedge median.
  - North side: the **Andaman & Nicobar Administration** office (a blue-and-white 5-storey building behind a weathered decorative wall) and a white gate-guard cabin.
  - South side: the **Fusion CX** glass tower (about 12+ floors) with a Maruti Suzuki Arena showroom on the ground floor.
  - Further east (`sv31_st13toring_h117`, at 22.5739, 88.4359): scrap and cardboard recyclers with tarp shanties on the north kerb, cars parked down the south side.

## 7. Water (canals and drains)

- **There is no canal or open drain along Street 2, Street 13 (PwC → College More) or Street 18.** The only drainage visible is covered: manholes in the carriageway and gratings at the kerb (`sv106_st13_h207`).
- The nearest open water on the east is a **large lake/fishery (bheri) east of Ring Road**, about 22.573, 88.4392 and beyond, roughly 500 m east of College More. Ring Road runs along its west bank. On the town side are the **Godrej Waterside / Infinity glass towers**. On the water side there's a promenade with a **faux-bamboo concrete fence**, coconut palms and benches ("Salt Lake View Point") (`sv30_ringrd_h000`, `sv30_ringrd_h090`, `sat_southeast_16z`).
- **Nalban** lake is further south (about 22.567). The **Kestopur canal** runs along the north and west edge (Chavipatty Rd / Biswa Bangla Sarani, see `d3_pwc_h000`, which accidentally shows Sector V metro and the canal). That's the north researcher's area.
- The game's water layer (`sectorv.json` → `water`) is the Kestopur canal line from about (22.558, 88.412) to (22.585, 88.436) plus a tiny 9-point pond at about 22.5822, 88.4239. Nothing in the game's south-east has water, which is **correct**. The Ring Road lake is about 475 units east of the origin, outside `PLAY_RADIUS` 330, so at most it could be a distant backdrop.

## What the game gets wrong / must have

1. **The Street 2 hawker strip is the signature of the start area.** It's a continuous row of about 150 m of tin- and tarp-roofed stalls on the **east** side (opposite TIU), behind a white railing, under big trees, with an arched gateway breaking it opposite the TIU gate. Not scattered single carts.
2. **TIU** is on the **west** side of Street 2: a grey podium, a white-and-glass tower of about 10–12 floors with a rounded glass drum on the north corner, a blue railing with university standees, and cars and bikes parked solid along its kerb. The main portico entrance is at the south end (about 22.5755).
3. **SW corner of PwC More**: a **peach-and-glass tower of about 20–22 floors** (the tallest thing near the start), with a blue hoarding along Street 2 and an auto stand in front on Street 13.
4. **PwC More is a T-junction.** The north side has a striped traffic-police kiosk and a signal, not a road. **PwC is a low white 2–3 storey building behind a white wall on the NE corner**, not a tower. If "PWH Tower" is meant to be a tall landmark, it's misplaced. The tall glass offices sit behind it.
5. **SE corner**: the small blue building with the chequerboard band, then the CSTARI compound with a red-brown fence and big trees for about 100 m. Then the **second stall row** on Street 13's south side (100–260 m).
6. **Burger King is in a podium under two curved glass towers** (Millennium City IT Park, about 13–14 floors), on the **north** side about 180 m from PwC More, with a grand staircase. The game's `burger` POI (22.57620, 88.43069) is on the road centreline. It should move about 30 m north onto the north footpath.
7. **Starbucks is not next to Burger King.** It's at the foot of an about 20-floor tower 50 m before College More (north side). **RDB Boulevard is not on this route at all.**
8. **Bose Institute** is the big glass atrium campus on the south side at 300–430 m, with a beige gate portal. It's the second big landmark of Street 13.
9. **Medians**: green pipe railing on Street 2; grass, hedge and palms on Street 13 west; a **white concrete balustrade** on Street 13 approaching College More. **Kerbs are blue-and-white**, not black-and-white.
10. **College More** is a 4-way crossroads (Street 13 × Street 18). It has the IEM glass building (SE), HDFC with red portal columns (NW), a 20-floor tower (NE), a median police kiosk, and a bus shelter with a curved roof and ad panel on Street 13's south side just west of the junction.
11. **Webel More is on Street 18 near Street 17**, about 300 m south of College More, not on Street 13. The game's stop positions for it match reality.
12. **No canal in the south/east play area.** Water belongs only at the far east (lake behind Ring Road and the Godrej Waterside towers) and on the north/west edge (Kestopur canal).
13. **Footpath kit to reuse**: trident three-globe lamp posts, blue barrier railings, pastel walls with iron railings, big canopy trees, rows of parked scooters, autos and totos at every corner.
